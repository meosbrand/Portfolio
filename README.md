## The 10-Day Compliance Sprint

A buyer sends a security questionnaire. You have no answers. The deal goes quiet, then it dies. That is what missing paperwork actually costs.

What lands in your inbox on day ten:

1. Fourteen policies mapped to SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria, ISO 27001 Annex A and NIST CSF
2. Risk assessment and register, scored, with named owners and review dates
3. Incident response plan plus a one-page runbook for phishing, ransomware and lost devices
4. Security questionnaire answer bank, the piece that gets stalled deals moving again
5. Gap analysis and roadmap, ordered by what costs you a deal soonest
6. Staff awareness pack in English, Nigerian Pidgin or Yoruba
7. Vendor review template
8. Thirty days of support after delivery

Delivered in 10 business days or you don't pay. Free revisions until it clears your auditor or your client.

Worth your time if you are a 5 to 200 person B2B company and customers have started asking for SOC 2 or ISO 27001 before they'll sign. Not worth your time if you want a rubber stamp without changing anything.

To be clear about scope: this produces audit-ready documentation. It does not by itself make you SOC 2 certified, which needs an accredited auditor and an observation window.

## About this site

A single-page site built from scratch. No frameworks, no build step, no JavaScript dependencies.

```
index.html    structure, content, and schema.org JSON-LD
index.css     design tokens and the full design system
script.js     theme toggle, scroll reveals, nav, contact form
llms.txt      structured summary for AI search and answer engines
robots.txt    crawler rules, including AI retrieval bots
sitemap.xml   single-URL sitemap
```

Built for search and for AI answer engines: Person, ProfessionalService, WebSite and FAQPage schema, a seven-question FAQ, semantic headings, canonical URL, and an `llms.txt`.

Design: warm editorial palette drawn from the Ààbò brand (deep pine for safety, warm cream, terracotta), Fraunces and Inter, light and dark themes, reduced-motion support.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/meosbrand/Portfolio.git
open Portfolio/index.html
```

If you move this to a custom domain, update the canonical URL and the `og:url` in `index.html`, plus the URLs in `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` and `llms.txt`.
