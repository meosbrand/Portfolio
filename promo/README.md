## Positioning

**The line:** Find it. Fix it. Prove it.

**The pitch:** I find the security gaps in your business, fix them, and write the policies that prove it to your clients. Then I put AI to work for you, safely.

**Why this angle.** Most security freelancers sell one slice. One is a pentester, another is a SOC analyst, a third writes policies. A business with no security team doesn't want to hire and coordinate three people. You've done all three for real: pentests for fintech, banking and pension clients, SIEM triage and IDS rules, and control mapping to ISO 27001, PCI DSS and NDPR. On top of that you build and red-team AI. Most freelancers can't credibly claim that combination, so every piece of copy leads with it.

The three colours from the site carry the same idea in the video. Ember is Find it (offence), teal is Fix it (defence) and periwinkle is Prove it (GRC).

**Who it's for:** small businesses and startups without a security team, especially fintech, SaaS and e-commerce companies whose customers have started asking security questions.

## Upwork

### Why there are two versions

Upwork says the profile video must show you, and Upwork staff have said profile media can't include an email, phone number or other contact details. The 60-second motion video shows your photo and has no URL, but a recording of you talking converts better and is the safe reading of that rule. The best setup is:

1. `meo-upwork-opener-16x9.mp4` (4.8s)
2. You on camera, reading the script below (about 65s)
3. `meo-upwork-endcard-16x9.mp4` (9.6s)

While you're talking you can cut away to sections of `meo-upwork-intro-16x9.mp4`. Keep your face on screen for most of the video.

| You're saying | Cut away to (seconds in the 60s video) |
|---|---|
| "I do all three…" | 12.0 to 21.6, the Find / Fix / Prove rows |
| "I also build AI…" | 21.6 to 31.2, the AI cards |
| "fintech, banking and pension clients…" | 31.2 to 43.2, the track-record terminal |

### Script to record (about 160 words, about 65 seconds)

> **[Who]** Hi, I'm Michael, Meo for short. I help small businesses and startups that don't have a security team get secure and stay that way.
>
> **[Problem]** Most security help comes in pieces. One person tests, someone else fixes, and a third writes the policies your clients ask for. You end up managing all three.
>
> **[Offer]** I do all three. I find the weak spots with web, API and network pentests. I fix them with hardening and monitoring. And I prove it, with policies mapped to ISO 27001, PCI DSS and NDPR.
>
> **[AI]** I also build AI automations that take repetitive work off your team, and I test AI features the way an attacker would.
>
> **[Proof]** I've done security work for fintech, banking and pension clients since 2022. Every report is in plain language, with a retest once the fixes are in.
>
> **[Ask]** So tell me what's keeping you up at night. Send me a message here, and I'll tell you straight what I'd fix first.

"Send me a message here" keeps the conversation on Upwork, which is what Upwork requires.

### Recording checklist

- Landscape, 1080p, camera at eye level. Look into the lens, not at the screen.
- Face a window so the light is in front of you, with a plain wall behind you.
- Sound matters more than picture. A cheap clip-on mic or wired earphones with a mic beats the built-in mic.
- Record each block (Who, Problem, Offer and so on) as its own take. Cutting between blocks looks natural.
- Use any teleprompter app with the script above. Read it a few times first so it sounds like you talking.
- Your current photo is a suit in an office. Matching that look keeps the brand consistent.

### Profile copy to match the video

**Title** (62 characters, built on the terms clients search for):
`Cybersecurity Consultant | Pentesting, ISO 27001 & AI Security`

**Overview opening** (search results show roughly the first two lines):
> I find the security gaps in your business, fix them, and write the policies that prove it to your clients. Most freelancers do one of those. I do all three, so you're not stuck managing a tester, a fixer and a policy writer.

Then three short blocks: **Find it** (web, API and network pentests, with CVSS-scored reports and a retest), **Fix it** (hardening, SIEM and EDR alert triage, firewall and IDS rules), and **Prove it** (policies, risk registers, ISO 27001, PCI DSS and NDPR mapping). Add one line on AI automation and AI red teaming. Close with "Send me a message with what your business does and what worries you most." Leave out email addresses and links.

### Running the test

- Run A and B side by side with the same budget and audience for 5 to 7 days. Keep whichever gets cheaper landing-page views or messages.
- Use 9:16 for Reels, Stories and TikTok, and 4:5 for feeds. Both read correctly inside the platform's UI overlays.
- Keep the hook as the first frame. Don't add an intro card in front of it in Ads Manager.

## Gaps only you can fill

- **A concrete result or client quote.** The site has none, and I didn't invent one. A single line like "found 3 critical issues in a fintech API, all fixed and retested in two weeks" would be the strongest frame in any of these videos. Send me one and I'll add a proof scene.
- **NDPR vs NDPA.** The video says NDPR to match the site. Nigeria's main data protection law since 2023 is the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA). Consider switching the site and the video together.
- **SOC 2.** The repo README still sells a SOC 2 documentation sprint, but the live site dropped it. If you still offer it, add SOC 2 to the Prove it row and your Upwork title. US startups search for it more than any other framework.
- **A sharper headshot.** The current portrait is 800×1000 and a little soft. A well-lit 2000px headshot would make the avatar frames crisper.

## Editing and re-rendering

The copy is in `video/index.html` (the `HOOKS`, `adCut` and `upworkCut` blocks). Change the text, then render again. You need Node 18+, Playwright with Chromium (`npm i playwright && npx playwright install chromium`), ffmpeg and Python 3 with numpy.

```bash
cd promo/video

# preview in a browser, real time
#   open index.html?format=9x16&hook=b&play

# music (bars count from 0, one bar = 2.4s), then level it: -18 LUFS for Upwork, -15 for ads
python3 music.py --seconds 60 --kick-from 2 --swells 5,21 --drums-to 24 --out /tmp/music-60.wav
python3 music.py --seconds 30 --kick-from 2 --swells 4,11 --drums-to 12 --out /tmp/music-30.wav
ffmpeg -i /tmp/music-60.wav -af loudnorm=I=-18:TP=-2 -ar 48000 -c:a aac -b:a 256k ../out/bed-60.m4a
ffmpeg -i /tmp/music-30.wav -af loudnorm=I=-15:TP=-2 -ar 48000 -c:a aac -b:a 256k ../out/bed-30.m4a

# video
node render.js --format 16x9 --cut upwork --audio ../out/bed-60.m4a --out ../out/meo-upwork-intro-16x9.mp4
node render.js --format 9x16 --hook a     --audio ../out/bed-30.m4a --out ../out/meo-ad-a-9x16.mp4
node render.js --format 4x5  --hook b     --audio ../out/bed-30.m4a --out ../out/meo-ad-b-4x5.mp4

# single frames, to check a layout quickly
node render.js --format 9x16 --hook a --stills 2,8,15 --out /tmp/stills

# Upwork opener and end card, silent
ffmpeg -i ../out/meo-upwork-intro-16x9.mp4 -t 4.8 -an -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -pix_fmt yuv420p ../out/meo-upwork-opener-16x9.mp4
ffmpeg -ss 50.4 -i ../out/meo-upwork-intro-16x9.mp4 -an -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -pix_fmt yuv420p ../out/meo-upwork-endcard-16x9.mp4
```

`promo/` is listed in `.vercelignore`, so none of this ships to devmeo.me.
