01 — About

Security shouldn't need a translator.

Three years ago I was writing Python scripts in college. Then it clicked: most small businesses are one ransomware email away from disaster — and the whole industry is busy talking to Fortune 500 CISOs instead of them.

So I picked a lane. I build cybersecurity that ordinary business owners can actually use — complete documentation packages (policies, risk assessments, compliance roadmaps) delivered in 10 business days, not six months, plus AI tools and clear writing that meet people where they already work.

My roots shape the work. Ààbò — Yoruba for protection, sanctuary — is my north star: security that feels like a trusted guard, not a vendor pitch. Technical rigor, warm delivery. I call it building things that actually solve the problem.
