Who I Am

The Polymath at the Intersection

AI, cybersecurity, content, and code — woven together to solve real problems for real businesses.

Three years ago, I was doing marketing and writing Python scripts in college. Then I fell into cybersecurity and realized most small businesses are one ransomware attack away from disaster — not because they're reckless, but because cybersecurity is expensive, confusing, and full of jargon.

So I decided to fix that. Now I create complete cybersecurity documentation packages — security policies, risk assessments, incident response plans, compliance roadmaps. What normally takes companies 3-6 months? I deliver it in 10 business days.

I also build AI tools with clean interfaces that people actually want to use, and design websites that make businesses look as good online as they are in real life.
