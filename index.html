Questions people ask


What does an AI and security consultant do for a small business?
An AI and security consultant finds and fixes the easiest ways an attacker could get into your business, sets up simple policies and compliance documents, and shows your team how to use AI tools safely to save time. For a small business, that means the essentials that make the biggest difference, sized to your budget.
Which areas of cybersecurity do you cover?
All three main areas. Offensive security (penetration testing of websites, APIs and networks), defensive security (monitoring, detection rules and hardening) and GRC (governance, risk and compliance: policies, risk registers and mapping to ISO 27001, PCI DSS and NDPR). One person can find a weakness, fix it and document it.
How do I protect my business Instagram and WhatsApp from being hacked?
Secure the email and phone number behind each account first, then turn on two-factor authentication with a passkey or an authenticator app, never share a verification code with anyone, and check linked devices every month. The 60-Minute Account Lockdown Kit in Meo's Store walks through every setting step by step.
Can a small business use AI safely?
Yes. Choose which AI tools your team is allowed to use, never paste customer, financial or password data into public AI tools, and have a person check anything AI writes before it reaches a customer. With those rules in place, AI can take over repetitive work like replies, reports and follow-ups.
Do small businesses need compliance documents?
Most need a few: a data protection policy, acceptable use rules for staff and devices, and an incident response plan. Clients, banks and regulators increasingly ask for them, and laws like Nigeria's NDPR apply to businesses of every size. They can be written quickly and in plain language.
